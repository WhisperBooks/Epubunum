Read along with the narrator.

EpubUnum joins a book to its audiobook. Each word lights up as it is spoken, so you can follow the text, find your place again, or learn how a language sounds while you read it.

It runs on your own computer: Windows, macOS, Linux and the Steam Deck.

EpubUnum is in testing on Steam.

From a book and a recording to one that reads along

  1. Choose the book and its audio

    An EPUB you own and the audiobook files of the same text. A book that is copy-protected is turned away; nothing is ever unlocked.

  2. EpubBuilder listens

    Speech recognition on your own machine hears the narration and matches it to the book, word by word. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  3. Open it in EpubReader

    Press play. The page turns by itself and the word being read is marked. The result is saved as an EPUB 3 with its audio inside.

Three programs

EpubReader
Reads and plays your books, at a desk with keys and a mouse or from the sofa with a controller on a television.
EpubBuilder
Makes the read-along book. It measures how fast your computer is first, tells you how long a book will take, and can work through a queue of books while you do something else.
BookManager
Sends books and audio from one computer in your home to the one that does the work, and brings the finished books back.

Your books stay yours

  • Everything happens on your computer. The listening and the matching are done by your own processor or graphics card. Your books and recordings are not uploaded.
  • Copy protection is respected. A protected book is refused with a message. The programs do not remove protection from anything.
  • A standard file comes out. The finished book is an EPUB 3 with synced audio, not a format of our own.
  • The book library is open. The part that reads and writes EPUB files is published on GitHub.
